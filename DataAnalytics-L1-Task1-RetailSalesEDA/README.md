# Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview

This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset using Python.

The analysis focuses on sales trends, customer demographics, product categories, numerical relationships, and payment methods.

## Objectives

- Inspect the retail sales dataset
- Analyze descriptive statistics
- Study monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Analyze customer gender distribution
- Identify top-selling product lines
- Analyze sales by product category
- Study correlations between numerical variables
- Analyze sales by payment method
- Generate business insights and recommendations

## Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
- VS Code

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Dataset Inspection

The dataset was inspected for its shape, columns, data types, and missing values.

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Mean, median, mode, and standard deviation were calculated for numerical variables.

### 3. Sales Trends

Monthly and quarterly sales trends were visualized using line charts.

### 4. Customer Demographics

Customer gender distribution was analyzed using a bar chart.

### 5. Product Analysis

Product lines were analyzed based on total sales and quantity sold.

### 6. Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was created to understand relationships between numerical variables.

### 7. Payment Analysis

Total sales were analyzed across different payment methods.

## Key Insights

- Sales performance varies across different periods.
- Product lines contribute differently to total sales.
- Customer gender distribution provides information about the customer base.
- Numerical variables show different levels of correlation.
- Payment methods provide additional insight into customer purchasing behavior.

## Business Recommendations

1. Focus inventory and marketing efforts on high-performing product categories.
2. Use monthly and quarterly sales trends for inventory planning.
3. Use customer demographic information to improve marketing campaigns.
4. Monitor payment preferences to provide convenient payment options.
5. Use regular sales analysis to support data-driven business decisions.
