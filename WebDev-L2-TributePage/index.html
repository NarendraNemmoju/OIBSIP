HIS STORY

A Life That Inspired Millions

01

From Rameswaram to Rashtrapati Bhavan

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Coming from a modest family, he developed a deep interest in learning, mathematics and science. His determination and discipline helped him overcome challenges and pursue a career in aerospace engineering.

Kalam played an important role in India's space and defence programmes. He worked with both the Indian Space Research Organisation and the Defence Research and Development Organisation. His contributions to India's missile development programmes earned him the affectionate title "Missile Man of India."

In 2002, Kalam became the 11th President of India. Unlike many traditional political figures, he maintained a close connection with students and young people. He believed that education, creativity and determination were essential for India's progress.

After his presidency, Kalam continued teaching, writing and interacting with students across India. His books, speeches and ideas continue to encourage young people to dream with purpose and turn those dreams into meaningful action.
